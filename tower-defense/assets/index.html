A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE

Welcome to Mosswatch

You are the forest's last guard. Monsters follow the sandy path toward your castle.

  1. Build: Pick an Arrow tower (60 gold) or Starlight tower (90 gold), then click a green +. Arrows strike one target; starlight deals splash damage.
  2. Upgrade: Click a tower to see its range and upgrade cost. Towers have three levels with increasing damage and range.
  3. Defend: Start each wave when ready. Defeated enemies give gold; enemies reaching the base cost health. Survive three waves to win.

You can build and upgrade during battle. Use the map controls to change speed or pause, and restart anytime.
